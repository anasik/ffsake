## Friction
1. **Cancelling a short task is a race.** `ffsake_task_cancel` replied "cancelling" even though job 6 then finished normally. Only the next status check shows whether anything was cancelled. To show a real cancellation I had to add a deliberately slow task.
2. **There's no way to unregister a task.** The Sleep task is still authorized and still listed in `.vscode/tasks.json`. Deleting it means hand-editing that file, and I'm not sure whether that also clears the stored approval.
3. **Cancellation used SIGKILL.** I couldn't tell whether a gentler SIGTERM was tried first. For `dotnet` that could leave build-server or testhost processes running.
4. **Status checks while running show nothing.** They say only `"running"`: no elapsed time, no partial output, and no way to wait. Every task needed two checks.
5. **The undo response labels are unclear.** `before_hash`/`after_hash` don't say whether they mean before or after the undo, so I checked the restored file directly.
6. **Re-registering replaces a task silently.** Nothing said an existing approval was being replaced. Approval was also granted automatically in this host mode, so I never saw what the approval prompt looks like.
7. Minor: on failure, xUnit split its output between stderr (the `[FAIL]` lines) and stdout (the details). Builds also created `bin/` and `obj/`, which the undo history doesn't track.
